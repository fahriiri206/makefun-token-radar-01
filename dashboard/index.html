What the snapshot records

Method & limits

Every estimate is a chain of snapshot figures

No names are special-cased. No market data is fabricated.

01

Peer comp

For each protocol, the model takes the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category. Fewer than three category peers fall back to chain peers, then all live peers.

02

Conservative haircut

The median is clamped to 0.05..4x and multiplied by 0.60. A launch is treated as worth less than the mature peer set, not more.

03

Growth & maturity

Seven-day TVL change is clamped to -50..100% and weighted 0.30. Time on record adds up to a 1.00 maturity multiplier over 1,095 days; unknown age uses 365 days.

04

Confidence discount

High confidence is 1.00, medium 0.92 and low 0.80. Confidence rises with a larger peer sample, a longer record, a reported 7-day change and multi-chain TVL.

implied value = TVL × peer mcap/TVL × 0.60 × growth × maturity × confidence

What this is

A scan of DefiLlama's sealed protocol record at the snapshot date. It is useful for choosing where to spend farming time and for comparing protocols on the same basis.

What this is not

Not a promise that a token will launch, not a live yield or eligibility check, and not a claim that a protocol has no token. The record may simply omit one.

How to read a row

Rank is the model's ordering; the detail note shows every input, the peer table and the risk register. Values are estimates, not prices.
